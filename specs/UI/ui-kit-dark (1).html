Overview

The Fool represents new beginnings, innocence, and spontaneity. This card signifies the start of a journey with an open heart and mind, stepping forward without fear into the unknown mysteries of life. It encourages taking risks and embracing new adventures.

Interpretation

When The Fool appears in a reading, it suggests that now is the time to take a leap of faith. Trust your instincts and don't let fear hold you back. This card reminds you that every expert was once a beginner, and every journey starts with a single step.

Reversed Meaning

In the reversed position, The Fool can indicate recklessness, poor judgment, or naivety. It may suggest that you're taking unnecessary risks without proper consideration. This is a warning to think before you act and consider the consequences of your decisions.

Symbolism

The Fool carries a white rose symbolizing purity and innocence. The small dog at his feet represents loyalty and protection. The mountain in the background signifies the challenges ahead, while his colorful clothing reflects his optimistic and carefree nature.

Искатель (Seeker)

The entry level for new users. Seekers can generate up to 3 three-card spreads per day and view reading history for 1 day. This role provides a solid introduction to the platform's core features.

Адепт (Adept)

Intermediate users with expanded access. Adepts can generate 5 spreads daily, access 7 days of reading history, and choose from multiple deck options. Perfect for regular practitioners.

Мастер (Master)

Advanced users with premium features. Masters enjoy 10 daily spreads, 15 days of history, and full access to all decks and customization options. Ideal for serious tarot enthusiasts.

Смотритель (Keeper)

Administrative access with unlimited spreads, 30 days of history, and full platform management capabilities. Keepers can manage users, upload decks, and configure system settings.
